First ninety days

What I would actually do.

Written from the outside, so treat it as a way of thinking. It would survive contact with your reality for about a fortnight, which is the point.

Weeks 1 to 3

Inherit the queue before changing it

Work the existing backlog as it stands. Meet every requesting stakeholder across Mable, HomeMade and the wider group and find out what they each think the Web Squad is for. Audit the content model, the integrations and the contract dates. Ship a few small things fast so the team sees a manager who unblocks.

Weeks 4 to 8

Make the intake legible

One intake path, a visible queue, an honest estimate on everything. Most frustration with web teams is not speed, it is not knowing where your request sits. Roadmap onto a single readable page, split between BAU and squad-originated, with the trade-off stated rather than argued privately.

Weeks 9 to 13

Point the platform at something that matters

By now the data says where the estate leaks. Pick the two or three that carry real commercial or customer weight, scope them properly, put cost, benefit and risk in front of leadership. Then run one end to end with post-launch analysis, so the pattern is established rather than described.
