The brief as I read it

Not a platform
project. An
operating shift.

A board-backed investment in a contemporary data platform, a contract runway to 2031, and members who have asked for better data submission, advanced analytics and an improved digital experience. The mandate is clear and funded. That is a good position to inherit.

The difficulty is not the roadmap. It is that Health Roundtable sits inside a commercial partnership while remaining a member-owned non-profit, and every digital efficiency can read to a member as distance. Dashboards and notifications can quietly replace the conversations that built the trust in the first place.

So the job is two jobs held together: run a disciplined product operation, and protect the felt experience of membership while you do it. Get the second one wrong and the first one does not matter.

The people who hold the member relationships need to be inside prioritisation, not shown a finished roadmap and asked for adoption. That is the difference between a platform members tolerate and one they advocate for.
